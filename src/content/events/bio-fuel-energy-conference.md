Biofuel & Bioenergy Expo Punjab 2026

North India’s Leading Exhibition for Biomass, Biofuel & Clean Energy Technologies

The Biofuel & Bioenergy Expo Punjab 2026 will be a premier industry platform dedicated to the fast-growing biomass, biofuel, and renewable energy sectors in North India. The exhibition will showcase the latest technologies, machinery, and solutions designed to convert agricultural residue into valuable energy resources.

![OpenAI Logo](https://biofuelenergyexpo.com/img/biofuel203.jpg) 

Punjab, being one of India’s largest producers of paddy and agricultural biomass, offers tremendous potential for biomass-based power generation, pellet and briquette production, bio-CNG plants, and sustainable fuel solutions. This expo aims to bring together industry leaders, manufacturers, technology providers, policymakers, and end-users to accelerate the adoption of clean energy and efficient biomass utilization.
